Can Dogs Eat Cauliflower?

Category: Vegetables · Verdict: OK IN MODERATION · Last reviewed: 2026-10-03

Why

Cauliflower is non-toxic in small amounts and a source of fiber and vitamin C, but the florets contain compounds that can cause gas.

Symptoms to watch for

  • Gas, bloating or mild stomach upset in larger amounts.

What to do

  • Feed plain, cooked (or raw) florets cut small, in moderation. No salt, butter, cheese sauce or seasoning.

Dose note: A few small florets; plain only.

Sources

  • AKC (American Kennel Club) — Fruits and Vegetables Dogs Can or Can't Eat
