Can Dogs Eat Dark Chocolate?

Category: Drinks & Sweets · Verdict: SEVERE — ACT NOW · Last reviewed: 2026-10-03

Why

Dark chocolate, baking chocolate, cocoa powder and semisweet chocolate have the highest concentrations of methylxanthines (theobromine and caffeine). Even a small amount can be dangerous, and baking chocolate is among the most concentrated forms.

Symptoms to watch for

  • Vomiting, diarrhea, restlessness, excessive thirst/urination, racing or irregular heart rate, tremors, seizures, collapse — can be fatal. Signs can appear within a few hours.

What to do

  • Never feed any dark, bitter, baking or cocoa-based chocolate. Treat any ingestion as an emergency and keep all such products out of reach.

Dose note: Highest theobromine concentration — small amounts can be dangerous.

Act now — emergency

This is a serious emergency. Contact your vet or ASPCA Poison Control (888) 426-4435 / Pet Poison Helpline (855) 764-7661 immediately, and be ready to say what kind and how much was eaten. Do NOT induce vomiting unless a vet tells you to.

ASPCA Animal Poison Control: (888) 426-4435 · Pet Poison Helpline: (855) 764-7661

Do NOT make your dog vomit at home unless a veterinarian instructs you to.

狗能吃黑巧克力（烘焙巧克力）吗？

剧毒 — 立即行动 分类：饮品与甜食 · 最后核对：2026-10-03

原因

黑巧克力、烘焙巧克力、可可粉和半甜巧克力含有最高浓度的甲基黄嘌呤（可可碱和咖啡因）。即使少量也可能危险，其中烘焙巧克力是浓度最高的形式之一。

需要留意的症状

  • 呕吐、腹泻、坐立不安、极度口渴/多尿、心率过快或心律失常、震颤、癫痫发作、虚脱——可致命。症状可在数小时内出现。

应对建议

  • 绝不要喂任何黑巧克力、苦巧克力、烘焙巧克力或含可可的巧克力。任何误食都应按急诊处理，并把这类产品放到狗狗够不到的地方。

剂量提示：可可碱浓度最高——少量即可危险。

立即行动 — 紧急

这是严重的急诊。请立即联系您的兽医或 ASPCA 中毒控制中心 (888) 426-4435 / 宠物中毒求助热线 (855) 764-7661，并准备好说明吃了哪种、吃了多少。除非兽医指示，否则不要催吐。
