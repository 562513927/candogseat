Can Dogs Eat Garlic?

Category: Vegetables · Verdict: TOXIC · Last reviewed: 2026-10-03

Why

Garlic is part of the allium family and is considered the most potent of the alliums for dogs. It damages red blood cells and can cause hemolytic anemia. Like onion, it is toxic raw, cooked or as powder, and garlic powder is especially concentrated.

Symptoms to watch for

  • Pale gums, rapid heart rate, weakness, lethargy, vomiting, diarrhea, collapse
  • anemia may develop over several days.

What to do

  • Never feed garlic in any form — including garlic powder, garlic salt and garlic-seasoned foods. Check labels on seasonings, sauces, treats and 'natural' pet foods.

Dose note: Most potent allium for dogs; powder is concentrated.

Emergency guidance

Contact your vet or ASPCA Poison Control (888) 426-4435 if your dog ate garlic, even a small amount. Do NOT induce vomiting unless a vet tells you to.

ASPCA Animal Poison Control: (888) 426-4435 · Pet Poison Helpline: (855) 764-7661

Do NOT make your dog vomit at home unless a veterinarian instructs you to.

狗能吃大蒜吗？

有毒 分类：蔬菜 · 最后核对：2026-10-03

原因

大蒜属于葱属植物，被认为是对狗毒性最强的葱属。它会破坏红细胞，引起溶血性贫血。与洋葱一样，生、熟或粉状都有毒，蒜粉尤其浓缩。

需要留意的症状

  • 牙龈苍白、心率加快、虚弱、嗜睡、呕吐、腹泻、虚脱
  • 贫血可能在数天内逐渐出现。

应对建议

  • 绝不要喂任何形式的大蒜——包括蒜粉、蒜盐和用蒜调味的食物。检查调味料、酱汁、零食和「天然」宠物食品的标签。

剂量提示：对狗毒性最强的葱属；粉状更浓缩。

立即行动 — 紧急

如果狗狗吃了大蒜，即使少量，也请联系您的兽医或 ASPCA 中毒控制中心 (888) 426-4435。除非兽医指示，否则不要催吐。
