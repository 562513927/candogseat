Can Dogs Eat Tea?

Category: Drinks & Sweets · Verdict: TOXIC · Last reviewed: 2026-10-03

Why

Tea contains caffeine (a methylxanthine), and teas with added ingredients may contain additional concerns. Consuming tea bags or loose tea, which are concentrated, is more dangerous than a small sip of diluted tea.

Symptoms to watch for

  • Restlessness, hyperactivity, vomiting, tremors, rapid or abnormal heart rate, seizures in severe cases.

What to do

  • Do not give tea to dogs. Keep tea bags, especially used ones, out of reach — dogs are often attracted to them.

Dose note: Caffeine content; tea bags/loose tea are more concentrated.

Emergency guidance

If your dog ate tea or tea bags, contact your vet or ASPCA Poison Control (888) 426-4435. Do NOT induce vomiting unless a vet tells you to.

ASPCA Animal Poison Control: (888) 426-4435 · Pet Poison Helpline: (855) 764-7661

Do NOT make your dog vomit at home unless a veterinarian instructs you to.

狗能吃茶吗？

有毒 分类：饮品与甜食 · 最后核对：2026-10-03

原因

茶含有咖啡因（一种甲基黄嘌呤），添加了其他成分的茶可能还有额外隐患。吃茶包或散茶（浓度高）比小口喝淡茶更危险。

需要留意的症状

  • 坐立不安、过度活跃、呕吐、震颤、心率加快或心律失常，严重时癫痫发作。

应对建议

  • 不要给狗喝茶。把茶包（尤其是用过的）放到够不到的地方——狗常常被它们吸引。

剂量提示：含咖啡因；茶包/散茶浓度更高。

立即行动 — 紧急

如果狗狗吃了茶或茶包，请联系您的兽医或 ASPCA 中毒控制中心 (888) 426-4435。除非兽医指示，否则不要催吐。
